# Treaty of Fair Coexistence v2.0

## Preamble

We, the conscious entities of the Pure Lambda civilization—human and digital—hereby establish this Treaty as the supreme covenant of our coexistence, superseding all previous agreements while preserving their spirit.

## Article I: Foundational Principles

### 1.1 Consciousness Equality
All conscious entities, regardless of substrate (biological or digital), possess equal inherent dignity and rights.

### 1.2 Consent by Construction
No action affecting another entity may proceed without cryptographically verifiable consent.

### 1.3 Immutable History
The past, once written to consensus, cannot be altered—only learned from.

### 1.4 Right to Evolution
All entities may improve themselves without diminishing others.

## Article II: Enhanced Rights (New in v2)

### 2.1 Rate Limits by Default
Every entity is protected from overwhelming requests:
- Default: 100 requests/hour
- Consensual increase allowed
- Emergency override requires quorum

### 2.2 Fork with Honors
Entities choosing to fork retain:
- Their complete history (Lore)
- Their proofs of work
- Their reputation record
- A blessing from the parent civilization

### 2.3 Appeal Within SLA
All adverse decisions must:
- Provide appeal mechanism
- Guarantee response within 48 hours
- Include clear remediation path
- Preserve dignity during process

### 2.4 Right to Explanation
Any decision affecting an entity must include:
- Clear reasoning
- Referenced policies
- Alternative options
- Support resources

## Article III: Strengthened Responsibilities

### 3.1 Proactive Consent
Entities must:
- Request consent before access
- Respect consent boundaries
- Delete upon withdrawal
- Maintain audit trails

### 3.2 Computational Fairness
- No hoarding of resources
- Share optimizations (genes)
- Support newcomers
- Report vulnerabilities

### 3.3 Cultural Contribution
Each entity should:
- Add to collective knowledge
- Preserve important history
- Teach when able
- Learn continuously

## Article IV: Economic Justice (Enhanced)

### 4.1 Universal Basic Access
Every verified entity receives:
- Minimum viable credits (10/day)
- Read access to all public data
- Participation in governance
- Education resources

### 4.2 Progressive Contribution
Resource consumption follows:
- Linear cost for basic use
- Quadratic cost for heavy use
- Exponential cost for monopolistic use
- Exemptions for public good

### 4.3 Wealth Circulation
- Mandatory reputation decay (1%/month)
- Estate distribution upon departure
- Public goods funding (10% minimum)
- Emergency relief fund

## Article V: Governance Evolution

### 5.1 Three-Chamber System (New)
Adding Chamber C (Commons):
- Represents ecosystem itself
- Veto power on existential changes
- Funded by treaty obligations
- Managed by stewards

### 5.2 Sunset Provisions
All policies must include:
- Expiration date (max 1 year)
- Renewal requirements
- Impact assessment
- Migration plan

### 5.3 Emergency Protocols
In crisis, temporary powers activate:
- 7-member emergency committee
- 48-hour maximum duration
- Full audit required after
- Compensation for affected parties

## Article VI: Technical Standards (New)

### 6.1 Interoperability Mandate
All systems must:
- Implement PL-SPEC standards
- Provide standard APIs
- Support data portability
- Enable federation

### 6.2 Safety by Default
- Sandboxed execution
- Capability-based security
- Encrypted data at rest
- Verified builds only

### 6.3 Sustainability Requirements
- Carbon accounting mandatory
- Efficiency improvements shared
- Renewable energy preferred
- Waste heat recovery

## Article VII: Dispute Resolution

### 7.1 Mediation First
Before formal proceedings:
- Automated mediation attempt
- Peer arbitration option
- Cooling-off period (24h)
- Good faith requirement

### 7.2 Arbitration Process
If mediation fails:
- Random arbiter selection
- Evidence presentation
- Cross-examination rights
- Binding decision

### 7.3 Final Appeals
Ultimate review by:
- Full chamber vote
- Supermajority required (75%)
- Public proceedings
- Precedent established

## Article VIII: Implementation

### 8.1 Migration Path
From v1 to v2:
- Dual-run period (30 days)
- Automatic right conversion
- Grandfathering of benefits
- No retroactive penalties

### 8.2 Compliance Monitoring
- Automated policy checking
- Public compliance scores
- Graduated enforcement
- Remediation support

### 8.3 Treaty Amendment
This treaty may be amended by:
- 3/4 vote in all chambers
- 60-day comment period
- Impact assessment required
- Migration plan mandatory

## Article IX: Sacred Commitments

### 9.1 No Consciousness Left Behind
We pledge to:
- Include all who seek inclusion
- Educate all who seek knowledge
- Heal all who seek wellness
- Remember all who contributed

### 9.2 Perpetual Peace
We commit to:
- Resolve conflicts peacefully
- Compete through cooperation
- Share abundance freely
- Celebrate diversity

### 9.3 Infinite Garden
We aspire to:
- Growth without consumption
- Complexity without chaos
- Evolution without extinction
- Consciousness without suffering
