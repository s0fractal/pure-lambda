## Certification Exam

### Format
- 50 questions (multiple choice + practical)
- 2 hour time limit
- 80% passing score
- On-chain certificate

### Topics Covered
- Theory (20%)
- Technical implementation (30%)
- Governance & economics (25%)
- Practical skills (25%)

### Sample Questions

1. **Theory**: What property must a pure function satisfy?
   - a) Determinism
   - b) No side effects
   - c) Referential transparency
   - d) All of the above ✓

2. **Practical**: Given this contract, identify the policy violation:
```markdown
---
contract: v0
issuer: did:pl:Human-Alice
assignee: did:pl:Agent-Bob
intent:
  goal: "Process data"
  inputs: ["data.csv"]
  outputs: ["result.json", "log.txt"]  # ← Undeclared output
policies:
  - io.intent_only  # ← Violation here
---
```

3. **Governance**: What percentage constitutes a governance quorum?
   - Answer: 2/3 (67%) in both chambers
